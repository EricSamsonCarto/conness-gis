# Yosemite Trails

Trails, peaks, lakes, trailheads and campgrounds across Yosemite National Park. Trail colors show the type of trail: hiking trail, footpath, dirt road or horse trail.

- **3D terrain**: On by default. The mountain button at the top right turns it off. Right-drag (or two-finger drag on a phone) to tilt the view.
- **Measure**: plan a hike by clicking along a trail. Distances are in miles.
- **Bookmarks**: save a view, its layers and basemap, to come back to later.
- **Search**: find a peak, trail or trailhead by name.
- **Filter**: open a trail layer's menu in the layer list to show only some trail types or difficulty ratings.

Trail data is from OpenStreetMap and can be incomplete or out of date. Check conditions with the park before you go.
